# ZEPTO-GROCERY-DASHBOARD

## ZEPTO GROCERY SALES DASHBOARD (Interactive Dashboard created using MS Excel)

Project Objective
Built a Zepto Grocery Sales Analytics Dashboard in Excel to track sales KPIs, analyze outlet performance, understand customer preferences, and support data-driven business decisions.

## Project Insights
- Total sales exceeded $1.19M across all outlets.
- Average sales per transaction reached $141 with an average rating of 4.0.
- Medium-sized outlets contributed the highest share of sales.
- Tier 3 locations generated the highest revenue among outlet locations.
- Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods were the top-performing item categories.
- Regular fat products contributed a larger share of sales than low-fat products.

## Final Conclusion
The Zepto Grocery Sales Dashboard provides a clear view of sales performance, outlet efficiency, customer preferences, and product trends. The dashboard helps identify growth opportunities and supports better business decisions through interactive visualizations and KPI tracking.
